OUR MISSION

Water changes everything.

771 million people live without clean water — and it's not just a health crisis, it's a barrier to education, income, and opportunity. charity: water exists to fix that, one community at a time.

Since 2006, we've funded water projects across 29 countries. And unlike most nonprofits, we can prove exactly where your money went — every project is GPS-tagged, photographed, and tracked from funding to completion.
